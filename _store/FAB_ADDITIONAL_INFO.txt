Six Blueprint skeleton characters on one shared Epic skeleton (93 bones: the UE5 mannequin's 89 plus jaw and pelvis helpers), with 48 armor modules, 12 weapons and 52 animations with 29 montages. Dressed characters are 16.7k - 18.9k triangles with no LODs; the textures are 1024x1024 for characters and 512x512 for weapons, all powers of two, on 2 master materials and 25 instances. Root motion is on the locomotion loops and the staggers; every other clip plays in place. Scale is 1 uu = 1 cm, with the feet at Z 0 and the characters facing +Y. The documentation is at Content/UndeadLegion/Documentation.pdf.
