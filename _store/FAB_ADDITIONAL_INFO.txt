Six skeleton classes rigged to the Epic skeleton for Unreal Engine 5.8: 48 swappable armor modules that fit every skeleton, 12 weapons on adjustable hand sockets and 58 animations at 30 fps that play on every character - two locomotion sets with root motion, 16 one- and two-handed attacks with heavy variants, a held block, a bow shot with string and arrow notifies, casts, taunts, hit reactions, staggers, four deaths, additive twitches and finger idles.

Each class is a ready BP_<Class> Character: capsule and movement, the shared Animation Blueprint, its default armor set and loadout, glowing eyes in any color. Armor pieces are leader-pose components on the body (WearModule / RemoveModule), weapons are Blueprints spawned on the hand sockets (EquipLoadout), and the fingers close on whatever the hand holds.

The demo map is an animation browser: pick a class, toggle and borrow armor, equip any loadout, play any clip, hold the block, toggle twitches, finger idles, root motion, turntable and eyes.

The Animation Blueprint is a flat layer stack - base loop, upper-body and per-arm layers, additive twitch and hit layers, finger layers - so attacks and casts play over a walk, a shield block holds over anything, and you can build your own state machine on the same clips.

Rigged to the Epic skeleton: the UE5 mannequin's hierarchy and bone frames with the skeletons' own proportions, plus jaw and pelvis helper bones (93 bones). Add it to your mannequin's compatible skeletons and every mannequin animation plays on the characters directly. Blueprint only, no C++, no plugin dependencies; 1024x1024 character and 512x512 weapon textures, every one a power of two, one Lit master material.

Clip lengths, root-motion speeds, the layer stack, sockets and grips and the per-class figures are in Content/UndeadLegion/Documentation.pdf.
