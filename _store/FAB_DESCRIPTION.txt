UNDEAD LEGION - MODULAR SKELETON ARMY

Six skeleton classes - Knight, Warrior, Archer, Assassin, Mage and Necromancer - rigged to the Epic skeleton for Unreal Engine 5.4 - 5.8. All six share one skeleton, so any of the 48 armor modules fits any skeleton and can be worn or removed at runtime. Twelve weapons attach to adjustable hand sockets in 11 loadouts, from sword and shield to a skinned recurve bow whose string follows the draw hand. One set of 52 animations at 30 fps plays on every character: idles, two locomotion sets with root motion, one- and two-handed attacks with heavy variants, a shield block, a bow shot, casts, taunts, hit reactions, staggers and four deaths. Additive head and jaw twitches and relaxed finger idles keep a crowd from standing still. Because the skeleton follows the UE5 mannequin's hierarchy, every mannequin animation plays on the characters as well. Each class is a ready Blueprint Character with its Animation Blueprint, default armor, loadout and glowing eyes already wired. The Animation Blueprint layers the arms over the legs, so the characters can attack or cast while walking and hold a block over any motion. A demo map with an animation browser lets you switch classes, mix armor, equip weapons and play every clip. Blueprint only, no C++ and no plugins, with a PDF manual covering the API, the clip list and the measured root-motion speeds.
