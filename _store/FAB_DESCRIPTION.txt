UNDEAD LEGION - MODULAR SKELETON ARMY

Six skeleton warriors on one shared skeleton for Unreal Engine 5.8: 48 swappable armour
pieces that fit every skeleton, 12 weapons on adjustable hand sockets, and one set of 58
animations that plays on every character. Each class is a drop-in Blueprint Character with
the Animation Blueprint, its armour, its weapons and glowing eyes already wired, plus a
demo map with an animation browser.

SIX CLASSES
Knight - plate helm, chest, gloves, greaves, boots, skirt - sword and shield, longsword
Warrior - chest, gloves, greaves, boots, skirt - axe and round shield, battle axe, mace
Archer - helm, chest, gloves, boots, skirt - recurve bow and arrow
Assassin - hood, chest, gloves, pants, boots, skirt - dagger, two daggers
Mage - hood, robe, gloves, greaves, boots - staff, wand
Necromancer - hood, robe, gloves, greaves, boots - staff, wand
About 170 cm tall, feet at Z 0, facing +Y.

MODULAR ARMOUR - ANY PIECE ON ANY SKELETON
48 armour modules as separate skeletal meshes on the shared skeleton: helms, hoods, chest
plates, gloves, greaves, boots, skirts, robes and pants. Every piece is a leader-pose
component on the body, so the Knight's helm goes on the Mage and the Necromancer's robe on
the Warrior, at runtime, through WearModule / RemoveModule / SetDefaultModulesWorn.

12 WEAPONS, 11 LOADOUTS
Sword, axe, mace, dagger, wand, heater shield, round shield, longsword, battle axe, staff,
recurve bow and arrow, each a Blueprint spawned on the hand socket at its grip. Loadouts
from sword and shield to two daggers (EquipLoadout). The fingers close on a held weapon and
relax on an empty hand. The recurve bow is skinned: its string follows the draw hand and
the limbs bend with the pull, and the shot fires an arrow.

58 ANIMATIONS, ONE SET FOR ALL SIX
- Idles: three general idles, a one-handed combat stance, two-handed, bow and staff idles
- Locomotion: two complete sets with root motion - a heavy shambling set for the rank and
  file and an upright set for captains and casters: walk, walk back, run, strafe left and
  right
- Combat: a stab and two swings per arm, each with a heavy variant that throws the body
  into the blow; two two-handed swings with heavy variants; a held shield block; a bow shot
  with string and arrow notifies; wand and staff casts
- Specials: three taunts, rally, cutthroat, summon and an area cast
- Two hit reactions (additive, over anything) and two staggers with root motion
- Four deaths
- Additive head and jaw twitches and relaxed finger idles, so a crowd never stands still
All 30 fps, seamless loops, one-shots that start and end on their idle.

EVERY CLASS SHIPS WITH
- A Blueprint Character: capsule, movement, the Animation Blueprint, its default armour
  set and loadout, glowing eyes in any colour
- The shared Animation Blueprint: a base loop, upper-body, left-arm and right-arm layers
  cut at the spine and shoulders, additive twitch and hit layers, finger layers - attack or
  cast with the arms while the legs keep walking, hold a shield block over any motion
- Root motion on the locomotion loops and the staggers, with the measured speed per loop
  in the documentation for in-place blend spaces; every other clip plays in place
- A montage for every one-shot, 33 in total
- Hand sockets you can move in the skeletal mesh editor, grips you can move in the weapon
  Blueprints

DEMO MAP
An animation browser: pick a class, toggle its armour, borrow any other class's pieces,
equip any loadout, play every clip by name, hold the block, toggle the twitches and the
finger idles, root motion on or off, turntable, recenter, eyes on or off. Drag to orbit,
wheel to zoom.

SKELETON
One skeleton for all six characters and every armour module: 68 bones, Mixamo-style names
(Hips, Spine, Spine1, Spine2, Neck, Head, Left/Right Shoulder, Arm, ForeArm, Hand and
fingers, UpLeg, Leg, Foot, ToeBase ...). Beyond a standard humanoid hierarchy it carries a
second neck bone, two jaw bones, palm bones and two weapon socket bones in the hands. It is
not the Epic skeleton and carries no IK bones; every clip is authored against it, and
retargeting to the UE mannequin is not set up in the pack.

NOTES
- Not rigged to the Epic skeleton. One custom skeleton, one clip set.
- The Animation Blueprint is a flat layer stack by design - loops as montages in a base
  slot, one-shots in slot layers - so you can build your own state machine on the same
  clips.
- No LODs: the dressed characters are 16.7k - 18.9k triangles.
- The weapon static meshes carry no collision; the characters collide through their
  capsule and the physics asset.
