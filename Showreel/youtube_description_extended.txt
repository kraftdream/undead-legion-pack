TITLE
Undead Legion Extended - Skeleton Army with Ragdolls, Cloth, Combat AI & NavMesh | Unity Asset Showcase

DESCRIPTION
Undead Legion Extended: the full Undead Legion modular skeleton pack for Unity plus the systems that make the skeletons playable - ragdolls, hit reactions, cloth, rise and dissolve effects, NavMesh movement, combat AI, an army spawner, an arena battle and a player demo.

Owners of the base pack can upgrade at a discount.

What's inside:
- 6 skeleton classes (Knight, Warrior, Archer, Assassin, Mage, Necromancer) on one shared humanoid rig
- 48 swappable armor modules - every piece fits every skeleton
- 12 weapons with configurable hand slots
- Glowing eyes in any color
- 58 animations: idles, two locomotion sets with root motion, one- and two-handed attacks with heavy variants, blocks, bow shots, casts, taunts, hit reactions, staggers, deaths
- Full-body ragdolls (11 bodies) that carry the killing blow and drop their weapons, with revive
- Physical hit reactions layered over any animation
- Cloth on robes, skirts and pants
- Rise-from-the-ground and turn-to-dust dissolve effects (URP Lit based shader)
- NavMesh movement on directional blend trees at the measured clip speeds
- Combat AI with attack modules per weapon, teams, health, damage and death events
- Army spawner: random classes, mixed armor under dressing rules, class loadouts, team-colored eyes
- Three demo scenes: arena battle, player demo, extended animation browser
- Unity 6, URP
